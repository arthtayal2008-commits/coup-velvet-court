THE COURT / BASE COUP

Bluff. Challenge. Survive.

2-6 players. Each starts with 2 hidden influence and 2 coins. With two players the starting player gets 1 coin. Last player with influence wins.

Challenges come first

Any other living player may challenge a role claim. Declare the target with your action. Resolve the action challenge before blocks, then allow challenges to a block. You may claim any role, whether or not you hold it.

To prove a claim, show the relevant hidden card. Shuffle it into the court and draw a replacement. The challenger chooses an influence to lose. If you concede, you choose an influence to lose instead.

Costs and influence

A successfully challenged action fails and its cost is refunded. A blocked assassination still costs 3 coins. A falsely claimed block costs influence and the original action continues. An assassination can cost its target 2 influence through a challenge plus the attack.

Revealed cards stay visible and never return during exchange. Choose which influence to lose. At 10+ coins at turn start you must Coup. Coins are public, cannot be lent or given, and hidden cards cannot be voluntarily revealed.

Online table timers

Turns last 45 seconds. Ambassador card selection lasts 30 seconds. Other decisions last 20 seconds. On timeout: Income (mandatory Coup at 10+ coins, random living target); unanswered challenge/block windows pass; challenged claims automatically prove if possible or concede; influence loss reveals a random hidden card; exchange keeps original cards. These are online-table rules, not printed Coup rules. Timers use the server clock and resolve while someone has the table open, or when a player returns.

Host removal

Only the host can remove a player. During a game their influence is revealed and coins returned. If removal interrupts a pending action or decision involving them, that action is cancelled, its paid cost returned, and play advances. Removed players cannot act or join the next round.

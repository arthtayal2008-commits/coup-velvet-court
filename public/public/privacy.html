Privacy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Coup · Velvet Court is a small, independent, fan-made game for playing Coup with friends. This page explains what it stores, why, and for how long. We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

What we store

DataWhyKept for
The name you type to join a table, your seat, cards, coins and movesTo run the game for everyone at the tableDeleted 24 hours after the table's last move
Private messages between two players at a tableTo deliver them; only the sender and recipient can read them24 hours
Account: username, nickname, a salted and hashed password (we never store the password itself), points, wins and games playedTo keep your points and place on the leaderboardUntil you ask us to delete the account
Google sign-in: a Google account ID, and your first name as the starting nicknameTo sign you in without a passwordUntil you ask us to delete the account
Match results (placements and points)To award points exactly once and keep the leaderboard honestAs long as the leaderboard exists
A one-way hash of your IP addressRate limiting, to stop spam and abuseA few minutes

Cookies and browser storage

  • coup_account: one sign-in cookie (HttpOnly, Secure), only when you have an account. It lasts 30 days or until you log out.
  • Browser storage (on your device only): your seat at each table so a refresh keeps you in the game, which private messages you have read, and your sound settings.

Voice chat

Voice is optional, and you join muted. Audio is relayed live by LiveKit between the people in your room, and it is never recorded or stored by us. Your browser asks before your microphone is used.

Services we use

  • Vercel: hosting.
  • Neon: the database for tables, messages and accounts.
  • LiveKit: live voice.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.

Your choices

  • You can play as a guest, with no account.
  • To delete your account or your scores, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with your username. Never include your password.
  • The game is meant for players aged 13 and over.
Coup is a game by Rikki Tahta, published by Indie Boards & Cards. This is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with them. The character art is original.
